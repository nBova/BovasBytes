## Ingredients:
- Pack of cherry tomatoes, halved
- Bulb of garlic chopped in half
- One red or white onion chopped in chunks
- One carrot, peeled and chopped in chunks
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Oregano
- Salt & Pepper
- Fresh basil
- 1 Cup of vegetable stock
- Optional: Coconut cream

## Process:
- Add your cherry tomatoes, bulb of garlic, onion and carrot to a roasting tray and coat everything in extra virgin olive oil.
- Next season the vegetables with oregano, salt and pepper. Toss them in tray pan until everything is well coated with oil and seasoning.
- Roast the vegetables in an oven at 350°F for about 40 minutes or until the vegetables are soft and have a nice color to them.
- Allow the tray to cool for about 10 minutes until adding the vegetables to a blender along with the basil. Be sure you squeeze the garlic from the bulb.
- Add in the vegetable stock (or alternatively water) and blend until smooth
- Empty the contents of the blender into a pot set over low heat
- This is where you can simmer your soup until you get it to it's desired consistency and taste. Adjust the seasoning to how you like it. I like to add a little coconut cream here to give it a lighter color and for the flavor.
- Once you're happy, it's done! Serve with a nice sourdough grilled cheese or crusty bread.
- Optionally for plating, you can spoon some coconut cream or oil on top of your soup in a little design like I did in the photo at the top of the page!
